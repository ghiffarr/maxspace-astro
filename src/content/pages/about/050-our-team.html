Maxim de Jong

Co-Founder and CTO
Maxim is widely recognized as the world leader in expandable structures. He was instrumental in the design and build of the first two expandable habitats to ever go to space, Genesis I and Genesis II, which were in LEO for almost 20 years each. BEAM was built on his architecture, and the expandable habitat is still connected to ISS. He founded Thin Red Line in 1999, and his expandable structures have been incorporated in dozens of NASA programs for space habitation, planetary Entry Descent and Landing and spacecraft shielding. His experience in design, testing and field integration of extreme environment technologies has taken him to 15 countries. Maxim holds multiple patents.

Nicole Stott

Lead Astronaut
A veteran NASA Astronaut with two spaceflights and 104 days living and working in space on the International Space Station and the Space Shuttle. She was the first person to fly the robotic arm to capture the free-flying HTV cargo vehicle, performed a spacewalk, and returned to Earth on the final flight of Space Shuttle Discovery. In preparation for spaceflight, she was a crewmember on an 18-day saturation dive mission at the Aquarius Undersea Habitat. With an MS in Engineering, she is also an author, artist, and co-founder of Space for Art Foundation.

Gerard Valle

Systems Engineering
A world expert in expandable habitats, he has led habitat development, testing and deployment for nearly 30 years.
