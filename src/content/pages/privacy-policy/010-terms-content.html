Cookies, DNT, and Global Privacy Control
05.

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • keep the site functioning
  • remember your preferences
  • understand how visitors use the site
  • support analytics and, where applicable, advertising tools (for example, YouTube or other embedded services)

You can control cookies through:

  • our cookie banner / consent manager, and
  • your browser settings (which may let you block or delete cookies)

If you disable certain cookies, some features may not work properly.

  • Do‑Not‑Track (DNT): there is no agreed standard for responding to DNT signals, so we do not respond to them at this time.
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC): where required by law and supported by our tools, we treat a valid GPC signal as a request to opt out of “sale” or “sharing” of personal information under applicable US state laws.
